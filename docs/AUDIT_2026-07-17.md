## Findings

### Identity drift (assistant-creep) — the main risk per IDENTITY.md
IDENTITY.md is explicit: aion is a HUD + app desktop, NOT Jarvis-as-personality,
NOT a chatbot primary UI. Several modules lean the wrong way:

1. **llm.py:22 SYSTEM_PROMPT** calls aion "an AI cockpit **assistant**" and
   defines a freeform chat + tool-call persona. IDENTITY.md §14 says freeform
   chat is *secondary tooling*, not the product. The prompt frames the product
   as an assistant — direct contradiction. (Not a bug; a framing drift.)
2. **jarvis.py** (whole module) + `IntentType.ACT` (core.py:43) = "act on the top
   Jarvis suggestion." Proactive suggestions are borderline "assistant theater"
   (IDENTITY.md feature-gate #2). Defensible ONLY if surfaced as glanceable HUD
   rows, not chat. Keep, but keep it panel-shaped.
3. **modes.py** persona-flavored mode docstrings ("Iron Man Suit Modes",
   personas per mode) — cosmetic, but nudges toward persona-as-feature.
4. **swarm.py / agent.py** — a full multi-agent + tool-calling loop lives inside
   aion. IDENTITY.md §18: "Not a single coding-agent loop (that's Ralph/OpenCode/
   Hermes — aion *hosts* them as harnesses)." Swarm/agent risk duplicating what
   harnesses should delegate. Verify these drive harnesses, not a parallel brain.

No hard assistant-creep in the render layer (grep of ui/ clean) — drift is
concentrated in llm.py + jarvis + swarm.

### Dead / untested code
- **Root-level scripts** aion_web.py, dogfood_agent.py, filetui.py,
  verify_voice_stt.py sit outside src/ and outside the package. Likely
  scratch/dogfood. Not imported by the package; candidates for docs/ or removal.
- **aion-slice4/** (separate dir /home/gio/aion-slice4) is a stale partial copy
  (aion_web.py + config/docs only). Confirm it's an experiment; prune if dead.
- test_term.py is excluded from the gate (pty-dependent) — acceptable but means
  term.py has no CI coverage.

### Technical risks
- **Optional-dep degradation works** (pyserial/joystick absent → deck disabled
  cleanly, tests still green) — matches principle #5. Good.
- **llm.py hard-codes FCM URL** (localhost:19280). If the FCM daemon is down and
  GROQ_API_KEY unset, chat silently degrades — verify the failure surfaces on
  the HUD, not just a swallowed exception.
- **swarm.py + agent.py** carry the most conceptual risk of scope-creep with the
  least identity justification.
